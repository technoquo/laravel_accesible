## What it requires

Text and images of text must have a contrast ratio of at least **4.5:1** against their background. Large text —from 18 pt (24 CSS px), or from 14 pt bold (about 18.7 CSS px)— needs at least **3:1**.

Logos, decorative text and text in disabled components are exempt.

## Why it matters

With low contrast, text is hard or impossible to read for people with low vision, colour blindness, older users or simply anyone using a screen in sunlight.

## How to meet it in Laravel

With Tailwind CSS, define the palette once and check every text and background combination you will use:

```css
/* resources/css/app.css */
@theme {
    --color-brand-ink: #0b1f3a;
    --color-brand-teal: #0f766e;
}
```

Pay special attention to:

- **Light greys** for secondary text: the lightest shades of the scale usually fall below 4.5:1 on white. On this blog `text-gray-400` was replaced with `text-gray-600`.
- **Placeholders**, which browsers render in light grey by default.
- **States**: the `hover` colour, visited links and error messages.
- **Dark mode and themes**: each combination is a new pair of colours.
- **Text over images or gradients**: add a solid or semi-transparent background layer behind the text.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing colours only for brand aesthetics, without measuring them.
- Checking light mode and forgetting dark mode.
- Light red error messages on a pink background.

## How to test it

- **Automated:** axe's `color-contrast` rule measures text on solid backgrounds. Over images or gradients it cannot calculate the ratio and flags it for manual review (`incomplete` result).
- **Manual:** use the colour picker in your browser's DevTools, which shows the contrast ratio, and check `hover` and `focus` states and every theme on the site.
